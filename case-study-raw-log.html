CASE STUDY · DATA-CAPTURE METHOD

Capture now. Reconcile later. Structure once.

Raw Log → daily QC → curated Airtable Journal.

Fast capture and durable structure are deliberately separated. During the day, text, voice and images can enter a persistent ChatGPT Raw Log with minimal interruption. A later QC pass reconciles the sequence into structured long-term memory.

Back to case studies

1 · Capture

Log the event while it is still easy to remember, without forcing structured form entry.

2 · Preserve

Keep exact quantities, times and image-dependent details when they would otherwise become irrecoverable.

3 · Reconcile

Daily QC checks missing records, duplicates, timestamps, corrections and uncertainty.

4 · Store

The final Airtable Journal remains understandable without reopening the original chat.

WHY IT MATTERS

Logging quality depends on friction as much as schema.

Writing every event directly into a database sounds cleaner, but repeated connector calls introduce latency, failures and interruption. Separating ingestion from curation improved usability while creating an explicit quality-control layer.

The architecture treats missingness and reconciliation errors as observable system behavior, not invisible housekeeping.
