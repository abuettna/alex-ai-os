A CENTRAL INTEGRATION LAYER

Why freddy plays a central role.

My Personal AI OS needs a reliable way to make longitudinal health and training data available inside the conversation. In the current setup, freddy is the main quantitative access layer doing that job.

CONNECTED SOURCES

Multiple systems, one query surface.

Garmin Connect, Apple Health, Intervals.icu and Runalyze are currently connected through freddy. That gives the conversational layer access to recovery, sleep, training, activity and other longitudinal metrics without rebuilding those source systems.

ROLE IN THE OS

Access layer, not replacement.

Garmin, Runalyze and Intervals.icu keep doing their specialist jobs. freddy makes the relevant records queryable from the AI conversation and exposes enough source context to support cross-domain reasoning.

WHY RELIABILITY MATTERS

Integration failures are part of the real system.

I am currently working with the freddy team on Runalyze import behavior. Runalyze is connected and activity data are returning, while the import is still reported as in progress. That distinction — connected versus actually current at metric level — is exactly the kind of problem the architecture has to handle honestly.

CURRENT SNAPSHOT · 7 SEPTEMBER 2026

Runalyze currently exposes 71 metrics through freddy, with many activity series reaching today. The source is therefore no longer a zero-data state, but the ongoing import means I am treating it as a partial recovery rather than declaring the issue solved.

That makes freddy more than a convenience connector in this project: it is one of the places where source availability, provenance and integration reliability become observable.

ARCHITECTURE

Specialist tools stay specialist. Context gets connected.

The system is a loop rather than one app. Measurement stays close to the tools that are good at it; the AI layer connects enough context to answer better questions.

REALITY CHECK

A green connector is not proof that every endpoint is current. Mirrored workouts are not independent observations. AI estimates remain estimates. Third-party APIs and integrations change. Those failure modes are part of the system, not something to hide.

WHY THIS PROJECT EXISTS

The problem is not a lack of data.

The useful information already exists across wearables, training apps, calendars, notes and structured logs. The hard part is knowing which pieces matter together for the question being asked.

The value is not another place to look at data. It is having enough context to ask — and answer — a better question.
