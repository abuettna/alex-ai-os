REAL-WORLD CASE STUDIES

Built for daily use, not for a demo.

CASE 01

A personal data lake without another tracking app

Natural-language observations are written into Airtable as timestamped facts. Meals, symptoms, ideas and context remain searchable without forcing life into rigid forms.

  • One conversational input
  • Raw facts preserved
  • Calculations only when needed
  • No duplicate logging workflow

CASE 02

Wearable data becomes useful context

Steps, floors, recovery, HRV and sleep are valuable individually. The bigger opportunity is combining them with events, food, training and subjective observations.

In development: Garmin → health layer → AI interpretation → longitudinal review.

CASE 03 — UPCOMING

Extending a specialist app through Apple Health

A case study on product feedback, health-data interoperability and how one missing data stream can unlock a broader AI workflow.

No personal consumption history or identifiable health records will be published.
