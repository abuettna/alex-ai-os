CASE STUDY · NEGATIVE ACTION

Sometimes the useful answer is no workout.

Good decision support should sometimes recommend less.

Recent training load, recovery context, the surrounding week and upcoming quality sessions can all point to the same conclusion: no additional structured training today.

Back to case studies

Recent load

What has already been done matters more than filling every empty day.

Recovery

Wearable and longitudinal context help judge whether more stimulus is likely to add useful work or merely fatigue.

Life context

Ordinary walking, family movement or easy cycling can remain ordinary movement instead of becoming another tracked workout.

Next quality session

The system can protect the session that actually matters rather than optimize today in isolation.

WHY IT MATTERS

Engagement is not the objective function.

A system that always recommends another task, workout or optimization can be actively unhelpful. This case matters because the recommendation was to leave the day alone.

“Do nothing structured” can be a successful output.
